FAQ

Questions, Answered

Everything schools usually ask before getting started with EDUNEXUS.

How is our school's data protected?

Every school's data is completely isolated on its own secure workspace — there is no cross-school access, ever. EDUNEXUS is built so your records remain yours alone.

Is EDUNEXUS aligned to the CBE curriculum?

Yes. Grading, performance (PLV) levels and analytics are designed around Kenya's Competency-Based Education framework, from Lower Primary through Junior School.

Who in my school can use it?

School admins manage learners, classes and faculty, while subject teachers capture scores for the classes and subjects assigned to them. A role-based permission matrix controls exactly who can see and do what.

What can we print?

Broadsheets, class registers and individual or bulk report cards are formatted to exact A4 print dimensions — ready to hand out the same day.

How do we get started?

Sign in with the credentials issued to your school. If you need help or want access for your institution, contact us at edunexus.system@gmail.com or WhatsApp +254 728 007 320.
